## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an auteur generative studio interface rooted in the discipline of bespoke print publishing, literary reviews, and cinematic screenwriting. Drawing inspiration from *Cahiers du Cinéma*, *The Paris Review*, *Monocle*, and heritage fashion broadsheets, the aesthetic treats digital prompt engineering and computational writing as acts of master typography and auteur curation.

The visual direction marries high-contrast editorial minimalism with literary formalism:
- **Atmosphere:** Archival, cerebral, deliberate, and prestigious. The workspace avoids generic SaaS neon gradients and frictionless blurs in favor of tactile ivory sheet stocks, deep printer's ink, and measured oxblood annotations.
- **Audience:** Creative directors, cinematic screenwriters, literary authors, luxury brand copywriters, and generative artists who require uncompromising typographic hierarchy and focus.
- **Design Movement:** High-fashion Editorial meets Continental Literary Modernism. Strict baseline discipline, visible structural rules, folio pagination, cataloging marginalia, and cinematic slugline metadata.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic discipline of archival print, hot-metal typesetting, and fine-grain press paper.

- **Primary (`#111111` - Printer's Ink):** Deepest carbon black. Used for high-impact editorial headlines, structural hair-line grid rules, and authoritative primary call-to-actions.
- **Secondary (`#5c0612` - Oxblood Muted):** Deep viticultural burgundy. Applied to manuscript proofing tags, folio stamps, screenplay scene accents, and secondary actions requiring literary gravity.
- **Tertiary (`#8b0000` - Crimson Annotation):** Expressive red ink reminiscent of an editor's physical grease pencil. Used sparingly for active states, prompt parameter focal points, and live studio telemetry.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#faf8f5` - Warm Ivory Press):** Unbleached rag-cotton paper base. Paired with pure `#FFFFFF` for pristine manuscript modules and `#f6f3ee` for contextual tool palettes and secondary panels.
- **Text & Stroke Hierarchy:**
  - Base Ink: `#111111`
  - Footnote / Metadata Charcoal: `#55524e`
  - Archival Stroke / Hairline Grid: `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.14)`
  - Elevated Plate Border: `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.90)`

## Typography

Typography acts as the primary architecture of the interface rather than mere content styling. The pairing reflects a dialogue between haute literary form and precise editorial production instruments:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Titleplates):** Classical transitional high-contrast serifs with dramatic thin-to-thick stroke modulation. Used for prominent headings, auteur manifests, and section indices.
- **EB Garamond (Manuscript Body & TextFX Output):** Elegant, humanistic proportions with historical provenance. Optimized for sustained long-form generative reading, treatment generation, and dialogue drafting.
- **Space Mono (Screenplay Elements & Technical Labels):** Clean, deliberate monospaced rhythm representing the typewriter ribbon, production script slugs (e.g., `INT. STUDIO - NIGHT`), hyper-parameters, token counters, and folio coordinates.
- **Stylistic Conventions:**
  - Screenplay slugs and utility labels must always be rendered uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`).
  - Drop-caps are encouraged for the initial paragraph of narrative modules.
  - Footnotes and system marginalia should lead with italicized Garamond numerals or bracketed monospaced folios.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model mimics an asymmetric broadsheet journal divided by physical printer lines. 

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column asymmetric system with `gutter-lg` (2.5rem) and generous page framing `margin-lg` (4rem). Main content often occupies an 8-column spine paired with a 4-column marginalia rail for prompt parameters, critique notes, and production metadata.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 6-column grid with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin` (2rem). The parameter rail docks into an editorial accordion drawer above the manuscript view.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single scroll format with `gutter` (1rem) and `margin` (1.5rem). Borders span edge-to-edge with inset content padding.
- **Structural Rhythms:**
  - Layouts prioritize deliberate vertical cadence over dense packing. Blank ivory pauses (`space-xl`) between generation runs are intentional to invite contemplation.
  - Column divisions must be explicitly punctuated by continuous 1px hairline rules (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.12)`).

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with historical bookmaking and editorial print standards, volumetric drop shadows and artificial blurred elevations are strictly avoided. Depth is conveyed exclusively through structural surface contrasts and tactile physical framing:

1. **Substrate Layering:**
   - **Canvas Ground:** The universal foundation is the warm ivory sheet (`#faf8f5`).
   - **Mounted Manuscript Deck:** Generating documents, cards, and canvas sheets sit on pure white (`#ffffff`) surfaces, visually resting flat upon the desk like freshly cut paper stock.
   - **Tooling Ribbons & Palette Panes:** Contextual utility trays utilize warm stone linen (`#f6f3ee`), establishing horizontal or lateral stratification.

2. **Micro-Borders & Structural Rules:**
   - Every card surface, split panel, and status tier is demarcated by a razor-sharp 1px border (`#111111` at 100% or `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.14)` for dormant states).
   - Key focal modules feature double-rule perimeter lines (a 1px outer rule, 2px white gap, and a 1px inner hairline), simulating fine publication binding.

3. **Active/Elevated States:**
   - Hover and selection states bypass elevation shadows entirely. Instead, depth is asserted through sudden typographic weight shifts, inverted high-contrast backgrounds (ink black `#111111` canvas with white/gold text), or an offset 2px hard ink ghost-drop (`box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #111111`).

## Shapes

The geometric personality is sharp, structured, and architectural. The default corner rounding is constrained strictly to micro-corners (`0.25rem` / `4px`), preventing the UI from feeling soft, casual, or consumer-grade, while avoiding the aggressive hostility of raw brutalism.

- **Standard Elements (Cards, Inputs, Buttons):** `4px` corner radius. This conveys the subtle natural trimming of heavy cardstock or high-gram paper stock.
- **Large Panels & Workbenches (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) maximum.
- **Pill Shapes:** Strictly forbidden for standard UI controls. Only historical circular stamps, archival wax seals, or generation status blips may use fully rounded radii (`50%`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (The Monograph Button):** Solid `#111111` background, `#faf8f5` typography, 1px border in `#111111`, micro-radius (`4px`). Typography set in uppercase `Space Mono` (11px, bold, tracking 0.14em). Hover state initiates a color swap to `#5c0612` (oxblood) with instant transition.
- **Secondary Action (Hairline Rule Button):** Transparent ivory fill, 1px `#111111` solid border, `#111111` text. Hover state inverts background to `#111111` and text to `#ffffff`.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Understated `EB Garamond` italic with a persistent 1px baseline underline offset by 3px. Oxblood hover.

### Cards & Generation Sheets
- **The Manuscript Plate:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background bound by a continuous 1px `#111111` hairline stroke. Top bar includes an authentic literary header: page/act numeral on the left (`[FOLIO. 01]`), date or screenplay slug in the center, and oxblood status flag on the right. Padding is strictly `space-lg` (1.75rem).
- **Auteur Output Tile:** Displays AI-generated treatments, screenplays, and verse. Features generous margins, leading drop-caps, and an inset citation rule along the left edge in `#5c0612`.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact rectangular modules with 2px corner radius. Built with `#f6f3ee` background, 1px border in `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.2)`, and set in `Space Mono` 10px uppercase.
- Status variants: Prompt variation counter badges feature a subtle dot prefix (`● 03 VARIANTS`) in `#8b0000`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Prompt Input Field:** Framed like a literary composition desk. Bordered with a sharp 1px `#111111` stroke on `#ffffff` substrate. Placeholder text styled in `EB Garamond` italic (`#888580`). Focus state replaces the border with an intentional 2px `#5c0612` stroke without glow or shadow.
- **Sliders & Model Controls:** Monospaced metric indicators positioned directly above thin 1px horizontal tracks with custom square (6x6px) solid black thumbs.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 14x14px sharp squares with a 1px `#111111` stroke. Checked state renders a solid `#111111` fill housing a crisp white custom typographic checkmark or solid black fill.
- **Radio Buttons:** 14x14px squares (retaining the editorial box aesthetic) containing an inner solid 6x6px concentric square when activated.

### Studio-Specific Components
- **The Screenplay Slugline Bar:** Pinned header with monospaced tokens denoting scene context (`INT. WRITERS ROOM - CONTINUOUS`), tone vectors, and token budget.
- **Marginalia Comment Pin:** Sticky note markers in pale parchment (`#fffdfa`) anchored to manuscript paragraphs with hairline diagonal tick marks connecting to editorial feedback.
- **Folio Footers:** Minimalist bottom bar featuring word counts, reading duration in minutes, and publication revision hashes formatted as `REV. 1.04 // CAHIERS ED.`
