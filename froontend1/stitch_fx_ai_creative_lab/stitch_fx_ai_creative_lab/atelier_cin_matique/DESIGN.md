## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-altitude digital atelier tailored for visionary film directors, executive creative strategists, and luxury maisons operating natively in the Arabic language. Moving decisively away from ubiquitous, lightweight SaaS dashboard tropes, this aesthetic draws from haute-editorial publishing, physical film production houses, and darkroom architecture. 

The visual demeanor is disciplined, quiet, and commanding:
- **Haute-Editorial Noir:** Deep, pitch-black canvases layered with textured charcoal surfaces, evoking velvet camera cases, high-definition editing suites, and private screening rooms.
- **Arabic-First Structural Gravity:** Native Right-to-Left (RTL) composition where typographic balance, timeline progression, and spatial hierarchy stem from classic Arabic calligraphic architecture translated through modernist grid discipline.
- **Cinematic Precision:** Micro-refined titanium borders, razor-sharp metrics, ambient bronze indicator lights, and slate metadata blocks that echo physical director viewfinders, calibrated monitors, and archival luxury lookbooks.

## Colors
The palette is built on tonal restraint, avoiding neon glows or hyper-saturated accents. Every shade carries cinematic weight and editorial warmth.

- **Foundational Neutrals:**
  - `Canvas Ground (#0A0A0C)`: Deep obsidian evoking an unlit projection theater.
  - `Surface Atelier (#121217)`: Charcoal velour for workspace panels, inspector drawers, and structural cards.
  - `Surface Elevated (#181820)`: Raised interactive tiers, modal containers, and timeline ribbons.
  - `Surface Subdued (#0E0E12)`: Recessed wells, timecode bars, and input backgrounds.
- **Metallic Bronzes & Studio Ambers:**
  - `Primary Imperial Gold (#DFBA73)`: Primary brand highlights, active director markers, and key status signals.
  - `Secondary Bronze (#C5A059)`: Focused frames, golden-ratio layout guides, and premium action states.
  - `Bronze Muted (#4A3B20)`: Subdued borders, inactive scrubber heads, and background track dividers.
- **Titanium & Type Grayscale:**
  - `Titanium Pure (#E2E2E6)`: Primary Arabic headlines, film titles, and primary readouts.
  - `Silver Muted (#94949C)`: Secondary body copy, script narratives, and metadata.
  - `Slate Dark (#52525A)`: Hairlines, grid coordinate ticks, and disabled glyphs.
- **Status & Production Accents:**
  - `Tally Red (#D64045)`: Live recording, render critical faults, and locked takes.
  - `Render Amber (#E09F3E)`: Processing neural frames, active generation queue.
  - `Archive Emerald (#3D8B6E)`: Mastered outputs, verified luxury deliverables.

## Typography
Typography is treated as high-fashion literature combined with calibrated instrument gauges. Arabic typography takes absolute primacy:

1. **Editorial Headlines & Dramatic Statements:** Driven by high-contrast serif structure (`Noto Serif` / paired with classical Arabic editorial styles). Arabic glyphs utilize classical proportion, graceful ascenders, and refined baseline connections.
2. **Operational Rigor & Continuous Text:** Driven by `IBM Plex Sans` (configured with its Arabic counterpart), providing humanist warmth alongside architectural clarity for script analysis, director treatments, and generation prompts.
3. **Film & Production Instrumentation:** Driven by `Space Grotesk` for numbers, SMPTE timecodes (`01:24:16:08`), lens apertures (`ƒ/1.4`), focal lengths (`35mm`), aspect ratios (`2.39:1`), and scene sequencing tags.

All Latin metadata mixed into Arabic text must be baseline-aligned precisely, preserving the natural flow of RTL narrative text while keeping numerical readouts tabular and crisp.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-column atelier architecture with strict RTL orientation. 

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize a calibrated 12-column variable grid flanked by a persistent collapsed director rail on the right side (origin of interaction in RTL) and dynamic inspector panels on the left.
- **RTL Fluidity:** Visual flow initiates from the top-right corner. Primary timelines sweep from right to left; playheads progress forward toward the left margin.
- **Breakpoint Adjustments:**
  - **Desktop (1440px+):** Full 12-column workspace with dual-docked panels, timeline spanning 100% canvas width, and continuous 3rem outer margins.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** 8-column layout. Inspector panel moves into an off-canvas drawer anchored to the left; right-hand director rail retains micro-iconography.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked mode. Video preview locks to a 16:9 fixed ratio at viewport top; controls, prompt engineers, and script notes expand below in contextual sheets.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through subtle tonal layering and razor-thin boundaries rather than dramatic blurred shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground color `#0A0A0C` with optional 2% monochromatic film grain texture for tangible depth.
- **Layer 1 (Atelier Workspace Panels):** Surface `#121217` bounded by a 1px solid low-contrast outline in `#22222A`. No exterior drop shadow.
- **Layer 2 (Hover & Floating Elements):** Surface `#181820` with a sharp, diffused amber undertone: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(223, 186, 115, 0.15)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Screening Overlays):** Surface `#16161D` with backdrop filter `blur(20px)` and an exterior hairline rim in `rgba(226, 226, 230, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
The shape discipline is deliberate and mechanical (`roundedness: 1`). Soft, overly rounded consumer radii are strictly rejected in favor of the engineered silhouettes of cine lenses, matte boxes, and darkroom apparatus:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, timeline tags, and status chips.
- **Card Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to media viewports, film reel strips, and modal envelopes.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted entirely to record/live indicators and audio waveform meters.

## Components

### Action Triggers & Buttons
- **Primary Director Button:** Solid warm bronze (`#DFBA73`) background with obsidian typography (`#0A0A0C`), font weight 600. Active states drop scale by 1% with a soft `#C5A059` border illumination.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent interior framed with a 1px border in `#2A2A34`, text rendered in titanium `#E2E2E6`. Hover transitions border to `#C5A059` and text to `#DFBA73`.
- **RTL Icon Positioning:** In an RTL environment, leading glyphs (such as generation triggers or play symbols) sit strictly to the right of the Arabic label.

### Form Inputs & Prompt Editors
- **Editorial Prompt Studio:** Recessed well (`#0E0E12`) with a micro-border in `#22222A`. Text input in titanium silver with high line-height for Arabic script reading comfort. Focus state activates a subtle 1px inner glow in `#C5A059`.
- **Parameter Steppers & Lens Sliders:** Custom tracks in `#1F1F26` with active track spans filled in `#C5A059`. Thumb controller is a sharp, diamond-cut 12px titanium square.

### Timeline & Sequence Markers
- **Scrubber & Playhead:** Hairline vertical marker in `#DFBA73` with an inverted triangle playhead top. Arabic frame numbers render using monospace numerical notation (`Space Grotesk`).
- **Scene Chips:** Compact rectangular blocks (`rounded-xs`) nested on the timeline track, separated by 1px hairlines. Active scenes present a subtle warm bronze border and charcoal velour background.

### Cards & Media Frames
- **Screening Cards:** 1px border (`#202028`), containing letterboxed media frames with embedded metadata tags positioned along the lower edge (lens, lighting prompt, resolution).
- **Hover Micro-Interactions:** Subtle zoom (1.01x) of the internal preview accompanied by an amber border transition, leaving outer layout grids unaffected.

### Toggles, Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Architectural Radios:** Squared design (4px radius) with an inset gold tick rather than generic round indicators, maintaining technical production discipline.
- **Track Status Switches:** Sleek horizontal pill sliders with charcoal backings and a warm titanium switch head that transitions to gold when active.
