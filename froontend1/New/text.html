The Engine

5 Techniques.
Infinite breakthroughs.

01
Provocation (PO)
Flip the Assumption
"If the opposite were true, what would we discover?" Turns constraints into opportunities by inverting the problem.
02
Analogies
Cross-Domain Links
Links the product to 5 unrelated domains — coffee ceremony, luxury perfume, fitness achievement — to generate unexpected angles.
03
Random Stimulus
Force Connections
Picks a random word, forces a meaningful connection. "Compass → Hydration is your internal compass to wellness."
04
Opposite Thinking
Extremes & Paradox
Pushes ideas to both extremes to find the irresistible middle. Generates the central brand paradox.
05
Constraint Reversal
Invert the Problem
"People forget to hydrate → People already hydrate unconsciously." Turns the problem statement into the solution.
