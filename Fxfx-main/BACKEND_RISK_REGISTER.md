## Risk acceptance rules

- A test file existing does not close a risk; relevant tests must execute on the intended tree and meet the stated criterion.
- Roadmap auth concerns are audit leads until reproduced against current source/configuration.
- No paid provider request, deployment, destructive data operation, or scope expansion is authorized by this register.
- Process-local storage and single-user operation are current constraints, not evidence of distributed guarantees.
- A risk may be closed only with linked, current evidence and explicit acceptance by the owning increment.

## Governance

- No paid LLM/API operation without prior approval and cost/risk disclosure.
- No destructive data operation, public deployment or irreversible security change without explicit approval.
- No source edits, commits, pushes or deployments without authorization for the specific action.
- Reclassify risks only with current source, test or runtime evidence.
- A risk is not closed because a test file exists; it closes when the relevant acceptance criterion is executed and verified.
